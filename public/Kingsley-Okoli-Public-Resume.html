Reliability method

Evidence contracts

Every verdict has an explicit proof requirement. A pass needs a real action and an observed result; logs alone cannot classify a failure.

Machine-enforced honesty

Deterministic hooks reject shallow checks, unsupported completion claims, silent skips, and false blocked verdicts at agent turn boundaries.

Durable autonomous work

Versioned skills, progress ledgers, checkpoints, and watchdogs let long-running programs survive context limits while keeping every item accountable.

Self-monitoring pipelines

Watermarks, structural validation, rolling backups, failure logs, and sentinels surface quiet decay in AI and knowledge workflows.
